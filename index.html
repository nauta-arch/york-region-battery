Why Buy Salvaged A123 Cells?

Tested & Graded

Every module we list as Tested & Graded is capacity-checked and balance-checked before it goes up for sale, so you know what you're actually getting.

Real Provenance

These cells come from real TTC transit bus battery packs — 12S8P A123 LiFePO4 modules, originally run in 16-series 660V packs — not unknown bulk-bin salvage.

Built for Solar Storage

LiFePO4 chemistry is one of the safest, longest-lasting options for stationary home battery backups — a great fit whether you're new to DIY power or expanding an existing bank.
