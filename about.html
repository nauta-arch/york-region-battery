About & FAQ

What Are These Modules?

Our core product is the A123 12S8P module: 96 cylindrical 26650 LiFePO4 cells, wired as 12 groups of 8 in parallel, connected in series. These modules were originally built into larger battery packs — 16 modules wired in series for a nominal 660V pack voltage — used to power hybrid/electric drive systems in Toronto Transit Commission buses.

Why TTC Buses?

Transit bus battery packs are retired for reasons that have nothing to do with individual cell health — route changes, vehicle decommissioning, fleet upgrades. That means a lot of the modules coming out of these packs still have plenty of usable life left, which makes them a strong source of cells for secondary, stationary use like home solar storage, even though they're no longer suitable for the demanding duty cycle of a transit vehicle.

How We Test & Grade

Every module goes through the same process: visual inspection for physical damage, a full voltage check, a controlled capacity discharge test, and a balance check across all 12 series groups. Modules that pass are listed as Tested & Graded. Modules we haven't run through the full process yet are listed as As-Is Salvage — visually inspected only, priced lower to reflect the added risk and work that falls on the buyer.

Safety Notes

LiFePO4 is one of the most thermally stable lithium chemistries available, which is part of why it's a good fit for DIY storage projects. That said, treat any used battery module with care: don't short the terminals, store modules in a cool, dry place, use a BMS appropriate for your series/parallel configuration, and don't mix Tested & Graded and As-Is modules in the same series string without testing the As-Is modules first.

Shipping & Pickup

Local pickup is available in York Region, Ontario, by appointment. Shipping is arranged case-by-case for modules — reach out before ordering to confirm shipping options and cost for your location.

Returns & Disputes

For Tested & Graded modules: contact us within 7 days of pickup or delivery if a module doesn't meet its stated specs, and we'll work it out case by case. As-Is Salvage items are sold as-is with no returns, which is reflected in their price.
